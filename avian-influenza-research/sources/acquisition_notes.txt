Acquisition observations, 6 November 2026 UTC

Official poultry landing page:
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/livestock-poultry-disease/avian/avian-influenza/hpai-detections/commercial-backyard-flocks
Its embedded Tableau dashboard was the source of the final event data.
The plain HPAI2022ConfirmedDetections.csv endpoint returned HTTP 200 but only
four monthly backyard-count rows, so it was unsuitable for this investigation.
Guessed direct detail-worksheet URLs returned HTTP 404. TableauScraper failed
to initialize against the portal HTML. These failures did not establish that
the underlying official data were unavailable.

The detailed crosstab export succeeded through the public browser interface.
Worksheet: X - Table by Confirmation Date. 2,300 event rows.
The first export used the inspected public helper from
https://github.com/casey891/hpai-dashboard/blob/main/tableau_export.py .
The delivered independent downloader scripts/download_poultry.py was then
executed successfully against APHIS; the final manifest records that retrieval.
No third-party poultry observations are used in the final analysis.

The wild-bird landing page specifies a direct CSV using data-csv-url:
https://www.aphis.usda.gov/sites/default/files/hpai-wild-birds.csv
That official CSV was successfully acquired, with 20,539 rows.
APHIS warns of historical sample processing and confirmation-date delays.

No test-negative counts, monthly susceptible-flock populations, weather series,
physical-premises identifiers, or genotypes were acquired. NASS and NOAA were
investigated as potential follow-up sources, not included as analyzed data.
