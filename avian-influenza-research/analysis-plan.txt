Prospective analysis plan written 2026-11-06 UTC, before computing outbreak summaries.
Existing workspace contained an APHIS-derived mirrored poultry CSV; its first records/schema were inspected only. Literature and official page descriptions informed these hypotheses. This is an analysis plan, not a registered preregistration.
H1: Recorded poultry flock detections per calendar day are higher in spring (Mar-May) and autumn (Sep-Nov) than summer (Jun-Aug). Winter is Dec-Feb. Fit monthly Poisson counts with log(days) offset, season and year indicators; use HAC covariance (3 monthly lags). Report overdispersion, year-specific contrasts, and sensitivity excluding incomplete calendar years. Tests are observational detection-intensity contrasts, not infection rates.
H2: Commercial and backyard-labelled records have different seasonal distributions. Report seasonal shares and Cramer's V; permute type labels within year (4,999 permutations) to account for annual composition. Rows labelled WOAH Non-Poultry cannot be assumed backyard poultry: exclude them from primary type test; show them separately and include only as an explicitly labelled broader domestic-bird sensitivity.
H3: The state composition of recorded detections changes across years. Contingency chi-square with 4,999 within-table permutations of year labels, and Cramer's V. No population-at-risk denominators; geographic counts do not estimate state susceptibility. Sensitivity restrict to 2023-2025 full calendar years.
H4 (exploratory): Higher wild-bird detections are associated with higher poultry detections in the next month after adjustment for month and year. Use log1p monthly counts, seasonally/year-adjusted OLS and HAC(3), and a first-difference sensitivity. Examine lags -3..3 descriptively; positive lag means wild detections precede poultry. No causal or out-of-sample early-warning claim.
Primary temporal inference uses complete months after the first poultry detection month and before the current retrieval month. Full available records retained for descriptive figures. Missing months are zero reported rows, not proof of no infections. Do not deduplicate poultry rows without premises IDs; flag exact duplicate signatures. Wild-bird record deduplication only with reliable identifiers. Record all date, type and missingness validations. Holm adjustment across the two H1 directional contrasts plus H2 and H3; H4 exploratory.
Seed: 8050. Save raw bytes, retrieval timestamps and SHA256 hashes. Freeze snapshots; offline analysis must not silently fetch new data.
